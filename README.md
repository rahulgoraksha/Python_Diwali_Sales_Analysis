## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset provides comprehensive transactional data from Diwali festive sales. It captures customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and product-level information, enabling deep exploratory and business-focused analysis.

This structured dataset helps in understanding consumer buying patterns during peak festive seasons and supports data-driven decision-making.

### 🔹 Important Columns

- `User_ID` – Unique customer identifier  
- `Cust_name` – Name of the customer  
- `Product_ID` – Unique product identifier  
- `Gender` – Customer gender (Male/Female)  
- `Age Group` – Customer age category  
- `State` – Customer's state/location  
- `Occupation` – Customer occupation  
- `Product_Category` – Category of the product purchased  
- `Orders` – Number of orders placed  
- `Amount` – Total purchase amount (Revenue generated)  

### 🔹 Data Capabilities & Analytical Scope

Using this dataset, we can perform:

- 📌 Customer segmentation by gender and age group  
- 📌 Regional sales and demand analysis  
- 📌 Occupation-based purchasing trend evaluation  
- 📌 Product category performance benchmarking  
- 📌 Revenue contribution and high-value customer analysis  
- 📌 Identification of peak-performing demographics  

This allows businesses to uncover actionable insights that improve marketing effectiveness and sales optimization.
